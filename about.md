# About

## Parker Ellwanger

**B.S. Electrical Engineering · B.S. Computer Engineering · M.S. Computer Science**

I am an engineer with a broad curiosity for how things work, from software and intelligent systems all the way down to electronics, embedded hardware, and semiconductor fabrication.

My interests sit at the intersection of **hardware and software**. On the hardware side, I enjoy embedded systems, electronics, chip design, semiconductor fabrication, instrumentation, and the challenge of turning a physical idea into something that can actually be built and measured. On the software side, I work with areas including **AI/ML, computer vision, automation, data and test pipelines, and systems programming**, with a particular interest in how software can make complex physical systems more capable and easier to operate.

I especially enjoy problems that cross traditional engineering boundaries: building an embedded system that needs to understand its environment, developing computer-vision or machine-learning systems that interact with the physical world, automating a laboratory or manufacturing process, or reverse-engineering a piece of hardware simply to understand how it works.

I am always looking for opportunities to learn something unfamiliar and to build things that expand what I understand and what I am capable of making. This site is a record of some of those projects, experiments, ideas, and rabbit holes.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy **travel, hiking, camping, skiing, reading, and exploring food and culture**. I have always liked the idea that travel is one of the best ways to broaden your perspective, and I share Anthony Bourdain's belief that food is a door into understanding a place and its people.
